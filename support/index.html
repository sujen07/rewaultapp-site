FAQ

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all your data. You can also email us to request deletion.

How do I disconnect Gmail?

In the app, go to Settings → Email Connectors and disconnect your Gmail account.

How do I sign in?

Rewault supports Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google only. There are no separate Rewault passwords.
